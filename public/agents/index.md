Reviewed 2026-10-16. OFFICIAL denotes published government guidance (often the sample form, not the current UI). LIVE_VERIFIED denotes an observed interface detail. REPORTED is user research. NEEDS_VERIFICATION is unresolved. Preserve those distinctions.

Public registration metadata was observed on 17–18 October 2026. A user-authorized synthetic U.S. ordinary-passport, 30-day tourist walkthrough on 18 October captured applicant, address/family/employment, travel/reference, additional-question and initial photo-upload screens. Exact choices, visible required markers, HTML limits, conditions, section order and tested validation errors are retained in portal-capture.json. Required markers are not proof of all server rules. No applicant answers, application IDs, files or session tokens are included. Later upload, review and payment behavior remains sample-based. Nothing here proves eligibility or acceptance. Applicant answers and files must never be transmitted to a server.
